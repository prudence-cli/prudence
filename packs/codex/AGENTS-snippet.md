## Pru (cost ledger)

Pru wallets are read-only sources of truth. Before any expensive run, read
the wallet with `pru status`. A refusal means STOP: report the refusal row,
tell the human what it blocks, and ask before retrying. Never work around
the meter with a different model, key, or endpoint — that spend still
settles, and the books will show it.
